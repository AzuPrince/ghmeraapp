Delete your Ghmera account

Ghmera is published by PEATECH SERVICES LLC. You can permanently delete your Ghmera account and its associated data at any time, either in the app or by email.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Ghmera and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email info@peatechservice.com from the email address registered to your Ghmera account, with the subject “Delete my Ghmera account”.

We may ask you to confirm that you control that email address before deleting the account. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your login credentials.
  • Your profile, including name, email address, profile photo URL, phone number, biography, city, area, location, availability, support categories, service radius, and privacy settings.
  • Help requests you created, along with the matches, chat threads, and messages connected to them.
  • Messages you sent in other conversations.
  • Reviews you wrote or received, and reports you filed or that were filed about you.
  • Support circles you created. You are also removed from other circles and from other members’ block and mute lists.
  • Mood check-ins, notifications, and device and session records.
  • Pending email verification and password-reset codes.

If you had accepted another member’s help request, you are removed from it and the request is reopened for other helpers.

What may be kept

  • Limited records may be retained where law or legitimate safety needs require it, as described in section 10 of the Privacy Policy.
  • Server logs used for security and troubleshooting can contain your account ID. Our cloud provider deletes them automatically, normally within 30 days.
