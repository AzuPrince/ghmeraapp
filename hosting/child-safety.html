Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Ghmera has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to all activity on Ghmera, even though the service is intended only for adults.

1. Prohibited conduct and content

Users must not create, upload, share, request, promote, or facilitate CSAM or any conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child. This includes grooming, sextortion, sexual trafficking, sexual solicitation involving a child, and attempts to move such activity to another service.

2. Reporting child safety concerns

Ghmera provides in-app reporting controls for accounts, help requests, participants, and chat messages. A user can open the relevant account, request, or conversation menu and choose the available report or safety-report action. Reports are sent to Ghmera's moderation workflow without requiring the user to leave the app.

Child safety concerns may also be sent to info@peatechservice.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate national child-protection authority first.

3. Our response

PEATECH SERVICES LLC reviews reported content and conduct and may remove content, restrict features, block interactions, preserve relevant records, suspend or terminate accounts, and take other action necessary to protect users. When we obtain actual knowledge of apparent CSAM or child sexual exploitation, we act in accordance with applicable law, including reporting to the appropriate regional or national authorities where required.

4. Cooperation and legal compliance

Ghmera complies with applicable child safety laws and responds to valid legal process. We cooperate with qualified law-enforcement and child-protection authorities and preserve or disclose relevant information when legally required.

5. Child safety contact

Google Play and authorized authorities may contact our designated child safety point of contact at info@peatechservice.com. This contact is maintained by PEATECH SERVICES LLC for questions about Ghmera's CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
